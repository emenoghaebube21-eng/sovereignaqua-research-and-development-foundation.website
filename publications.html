PUBLICATION TYPES

Organize Knowledge by Use

As the repository grows, resources can be grouped by their purpose and evidence level.

Scientific and technical

  • Peer-reviewed or formally reviewed research
  • Technical reports and working papers
  • Field studies and documented case studies
  • Research methods and data documentation

Public and educational

  • Educational materials and learning resources
  • Policy and programme briefs
  • Community-facing research communication
  • Institutional and annual reporting materials
PUBLICATION STANDARD

Evidence Before Claims

Published material should make it possible for readers to understand what is established, what is under study, what is proposed and what remains subject to further validation.

Attribution

Identify authors, contributors, source materials and relevant institutional associations where appropriate.

Review

Apply an appropriate review process before presenting research or programme findings as established outputs.

Version Control

Maintain publication dates, revisions and document status so readers can distinguish current material from earlier versions.
