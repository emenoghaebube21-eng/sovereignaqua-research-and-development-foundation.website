ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 2, 2026

We aim to make the Foundation website usable and understandable for visitors with different abilities, devices and assistive technologies.

Our approach

We use semantic HTML, readable content, responsive layouts, keyboard-accessible controls and descriptive labels where practical. We aim to align our digital work with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Current limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Some legacy or third-party content may not yet meet every accessibility criterion. We review and improve the site as pages and services are updated.

Report an accessibility issue

If you encounter a barrier, please use the Contact page and describe the page, issue and device or assistive technology involved when possible.
