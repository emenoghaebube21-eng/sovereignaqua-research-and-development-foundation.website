Implementation Binder Register

The next controlled layer after the architecture index: a register that converts the working structural model into evidence-backed implementation records without treating proposed relationships as completed legal facts.

Gate: No entity, asset, funding flow, inter-entity service, IP transfer or PMA interface should be marked implemented until the underlying authority and evidence have been reviewed.
