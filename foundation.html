Research-to-Impact Model

Evidence first. Implementation second.

Define the problem, conduct or coordinate research, validate findings, develop a pilot, measure outcomes and publish appropriate evidence.

1. Research

Problem definition, baseline assessment, field observation and technical analysis.

2. Validate

Peer review, testing, monitoring and responsible assessment of findings.

3. Apply

Pilot implementation, measurement, knowledge transfer and scale-up where justified.
