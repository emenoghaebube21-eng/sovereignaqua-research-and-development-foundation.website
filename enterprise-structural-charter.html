Enterprise Structural Charter

A working master structure for governance, ownership, asset control and financial-flow discipline across the SovereignAqua enterprise ecosystem.

View Structural HierarchyMaster Asset ScheduleAllocation Framework
A. Purpose

Formalize the architecture before execution.

The charter establishes the intended relationship between the charitable foundation, management layer, asset-holding layer and operating functions so that ownership, use, transactions and accountability can be documented separately.

Preserve mission integrity

Keep the Foundation's charitable, research, educational and humanitarian mission distinguishable from commercial or operational activity.

Separate assets from operations

Identify where assets are held, which entity uses them and what written instrument governs that use.

Control financial flows

Require traceable authority, documented consideration or allocation, accounting treatment and reconciliation for inter-entity activity.

C. Structural Intent

Five control objectives

01 — Mission

Preserve the Foundation's public-benefit purpose and board oversight.

02 — Separation

Separate charitable assets, commercial operations and support functions where the facts and governing documents require it.

03 — Liability

Use distinct entities, contracts, records and insurance structures as appropriate to manage operational exposure.

04 — Compliance

Review related-party transactions, private benefit, valuation, tax and regulatory consequences before execution.

05 — Reinvestment

Any transfer of value involving the Foundation must be structured and reviewed so it serves lawful charitable purposes and does not create prohibited private benefit or inurement.

06 — Evidence

No material ownership, transfer, lease, license, sale or service relationship should rely solely on an informal understanding.

Federal tax note: the IRS states that a 501(c)(3) organization must operate for exempt purposes and may not allow its net earnings to inure to private shareholders or individuals. The actual tax treatment of any SovereignAqua entity relationship depends on the entities, documents, transactions and facts as implemented.
