Control Protocol

1 · Verify

Confirm the actual holder, title, registration, possession or contractual right.

2 · Value

Document the valuation or pricing basis before a material transfer or related transaction.

3 · Authorize

Confirm the entity and officer, trustee, board or member authority to execute the transaction.

4 · Reconcile

Match the executed instrument to the asset ledger, transaction register and accounting records.

Related-party control: If the Foundation becomes subject to Form 990 reporting, actual relationships and transaction facts must be assessed under the applicable IRS rules. Schedule R can require reporting of related organizations and certain transactions with them; this register is therefore an internal control tool, not a determination of tax status.
