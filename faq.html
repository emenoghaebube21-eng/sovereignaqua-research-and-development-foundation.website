GENERAL QUESTIONS

About SovereignAqua

What is SovereignAqua Research & Development Foundation?

SovereignAqua is presented as a research and development foundation focused on scientific research, environmental stewardship, innovation, education and sustainable development.

How can I become a member?

Visit the Membership Application page and complete the application form. Applications are reviewed and applicants receive information about the next steps.

Can I support the Foundation?

Individuals, organizations, researchers and institutions can explore collaboration, sponsorship, volunteering or financial-support pathways. Financial contributions should use only a verified payment destination.

Does the Foundation publish research?

The Publications section is intended for research papers, reports, educational resources and other materials that have been approved for publication.

How public information is handled

Verified information first.
Planned activities, funding applications, developing partnerships and proposed programmes should be clearly distinguished from confirmed institutional results. A project or funding pathway appearing on the website does not by itself establish an award, approval, endorsement or partnership.
