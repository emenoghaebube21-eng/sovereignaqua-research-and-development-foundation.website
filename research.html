RESEARCH PATHWAY

From Questions to Responsible Application

The Foundation's research framework is designed to keep inquiry, validation, collaboration and application connected while allowing each programme to mature at its own pace.

01

Discover

Define research questions, identify evidence needs and establish the scope of inquiry.

02

Validate

Evaluate methods, evidence, assumptions and findings before presenting conclusions.

03

Apply

Translate validated knowledge into appropriate prototypes, programmes, education or partnerships.

04

Measure

Document results, lessons, limitations and next steps so future decisions remain evidence-based.

INNOVATION

Research Connected to Practical Solutions

Innovation activities are intended to connect researchers, communities, institutions and responsible technology partners around testable solutions.

Scientific Inquiry

Investigate defined questions through appropriate research methods and documented evidence.

Prototype & Test

Translate promising ideas into practical concepts that can be evaluated before wider implementation.

Collaborate

Build interdisciplinary and institutional partnerships around clearly defined objectives and responsibilities.
