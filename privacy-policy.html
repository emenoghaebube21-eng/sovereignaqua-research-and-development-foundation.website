Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This website is operated by the SovereignAqua Research & Development Foundation. This policy describes the general approach to information submitted through this static website.

Information you provide

If you contact the Foundation or prepare a membership application through this site, information such as your name, email address, organization and message may be included in an email you choose to send. The website does not currently provide a server-side application database.

How information is used

Information voluntarily sent to the Foundation may be used to respond to inquiries, review membership requests, coordinate research or partnership discussions, and provide requested communications.

Cookies and analytics

This site should not be understood to provide a guarantee about third-party services, browser storage or hosting-provider logs. Any future analytics, forms or third-party integrations should be documented here when implemented.

Data sharing

The Foundation should not disclose submitted information except where necessary to respond to the request, operate applicable services, comply with legal obligations, or protect legitimate institutional interests.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
