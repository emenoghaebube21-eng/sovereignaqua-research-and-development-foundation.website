Implementation Architecture

Entity, Asset & Transaction Allocation Framework

A proposed implementation register for translating the SovereignAqua organizational architecture into clear legal, accounting and operational records.

Status: Working framework. The allocations below are proposed planning positions, not statements that transfers, ownership, contracts or entity formations have already occurred. Each item requires documentary and jurisdiction-specific verification before implementation.

Purpose of the schedule

This framework is the implementation bridge between the organizational architecture and the records that will govern assets, inventory, revenue, expenses and inter-entity transactions. Its purpose is to make every material movement traceable to a defined holder, operating function and written instrument.

Separate functions

Production, processing, distribution, retail/food service, research and finance can be represented as distinct functions or entities according to the final legal structure.

Trace assets

Identify who owns, leases, holds or controls each asset and document any assignment, lease, license or other transfer.

Document transactions

Use written agreements, harvest records, invoices, purchase/sale records and accounting entries to support related-party and inter-entity transactions.

Control Gates

Implementation sequence

Freeze the architecture

Confirm the final organizational chart, operating functions and intended entity/division boundaries before transferring assets or revenue streams.

Complete the entity and asset schedule

Replace TBD fields with verified ownership, lease rights, entity names, jurisdictions and responsible records custodians.

Build the transaction matrix

For each inter-entity transaction, specify the trigger, price methodology, invoice/record, approval authority, accounting treatment and supporting agreement.

Apply compliance gates

Confirm licensing, tax, nonprofit/private-benefit, food-processing, environmental, employment and other regulatory requirements applicable to the actual activity and jurisdiction.

Pilot before scaling

Run a controlled transaction cycle, reconcile inventory and accounting records, review related-party documentation and correct gaps before expansion.

Transaction documentation checklist

  • Current title, ownership or lease evidence
  • Entity formation and authority records
  • Asset assignment or lease agreement
  • Inventory opening balance and valuation policy
  • Harvest record and chain-of-custody record
  • Purchase/sale agreement and invoice
  • Equipment ownership, lease or use agreement
  • Supply and distribution agreements
  • Trademark/IP ownership and license terms
  • Research scope, deliverables and IP provisions
  • Capitalization and loan documentation where applicable
  • Board/trustee/management approvals as required
  • Related-party disclosure and review record
  • Accounting entry and reconciliation trail
  • Regulatory permits and compliance evidence
  • Retention and document-control schedule

Architecture before transactions

The framework should be treated as a control document: establish who performs the activity, who owns the relevant asset, who earns the revenue, who bears the expense and what written instrument supports the transaction. Implementation should follow verification rather than assumptions.
