Giving Principles

Built for accountability

Choose your purpose.
Designate a programme or use the general fund where permitted by the Foundation's donation policy.
Use a verified checkout.
Payments should be processed through an approved payment provider rather than through credentials stored in the public website.
Receive confirmation.
A production implementation should record the transaction reference and provide appropriate donor confirmation or receipt.
Report responsibly.
Programme and financial reporting should distinguish verified results from proposed or developing activities.

Major gifts, grants and partnerships
