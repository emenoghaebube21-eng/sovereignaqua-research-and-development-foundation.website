ASSET ENROLMENT

About the Programme

The Asset Enrolment Programme provides a structured process for documenting eligible assets submitted to the Foundation for participation in approved programmes.

Applicants should review all eligibility requirements and provide accurate supporting documentation during the enrolment process.

Submission of an enrolment application does not, by itself, constitute acceptance, verification, valuation, transfer of ownership, custody, or approval of an asset. Any such action is subject to applicable programme requirements and formal review.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants should prepare the information necessary for the Foundation's review process.

Identity Verification

Provide valid identification where required by the applicable programme or review process.

Ownership Documentation

Submit documentation demonstrating your authority, ownership, or other lawful interest where applicable.

Supporting Records

Include requested supporting records relevant to the asset and the applicable programme.

PROCESS

BEFORE SUBMITTING

Prepare Your Documents

Providing complete and accurate documentation can help reduce unnecessary delays during review.

  • Government-issued identification, where applicable.
  • Proof of ownership or lawful authority.
  • Supporting records relevant to the asset.
  • Completed enrolment information.
  • Additional documentation if requested during review.

Do not submit sensitive information unless the Foundation specifically requests it through an authorized submission channel.

DOCUMENTATION

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the review process take?

Review times vary depending on the completeness of the submitted information and the nature of the application.

Can I update my application?

Yes. If additional information is requested, you may submit updates through the designated review process.

Does submitting an asset mean it has been accepted?

No. Submission initiates a review process. Acceptance, eligibility, verification, or participation in a programme remains subject to the applicable requirements and review.

What if I need assistance?

Contact the Foundation through the official Contact Centre for questions concerning eligibility, documentation, or the enrolment process.
