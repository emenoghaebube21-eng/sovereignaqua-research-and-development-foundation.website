Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar browser technologies may be used on the Foundation website.

Current website

The current Foundation pages are primarily static HTML. No Foundation-owned cookie consent or analytics system is represented here unless a specific service is later integrated.

Third-party services

Future integrations such as analytics, payment processing, embedded media or other third-party services may set their own cookies or storage. Their applicable policies should be reviewed before use.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to inspect, block or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking cookies can affect websites that depend on them.
