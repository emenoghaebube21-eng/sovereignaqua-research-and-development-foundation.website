OUR GOVERNANCE

Strong Governance Builds Strong Institutions

The SovereignAqua Research & Development Foundation is committed to transparent governance, responsible stewardship of resources, ethical leadership, and compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Governance responsibilities should be exercised through clearly defined roles, documented decisions, appropriate oversight, and responsible institutional administration.

LEADERSHIP

Board & Executive Leadership

Leadership roles are structured to provide strategic direction, operational administration, research oversight, and institutional accountability.

GOVERNANCE

Board Chair

Provides strategic leadership and governance oversight.

EXECUTIVE

Executive Director

Leads day-to-day operations and implementation of approved programmes.

RESEARCH

Research Director

Guides research priorities and scientific collaboration.

Leadership titles and responsibilities should be read together with the Foundation's governing documents and applicable organizational requirements.
