Controlled Record · APR-REG

Approval Record Register

Final governance gate for material entity, asset, agreement, transaction, funding and compliance decisions. The register records who decided, what was reviewed, what conditions apply and whether the item is approved, deferred or rejected.

Approval boundary: An entry in this register is not an approval by itself. A decision becomes effective only when made by the competent authority under the applicable governing documents and supported by an identifiable resolution, consent, delegated authority or other valid record.
